# Responsible research and submission scope

This is a research prototype, not a clinical product or a validated compound-discovery result. Administrative eligibility, registration and submission deadlines remain the team’s responsibility; this file does not certify them.

## Evidence boundaries

- ENPKG extract chemistry and archived assay readouts are measured inputs. Compound identities are mass-spectrum annotations, not confirmed structures; model attribution does not establish causation.
- Benchmark pharmacology is simulated over real co-occurrence structure. Report strata, policy, denominator and uncertainty with results. The hard-stratum scalar potency result ties strongest-first at 19%; it does not establish superiority.
- Selectivity-aware waste reduction is a simulation result under assumed cytotoxic prevalence. It is not a measured saving in laboratory time or money.
- The workbench uses expected nomination utility (`decouple`); adaptive contrast is a separate benchmark policy. Scalar threshold nominations and set-valued verdicts have separate evaluations.
- A joint verdict means the model favours joint contribution. It does not prove synergy or establish what fractionation will do.
- The L6 host-cell assay is not a human clinical safety test. Purified-compound experiments and independent validation are future work.
